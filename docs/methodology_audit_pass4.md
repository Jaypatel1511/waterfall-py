## HIGH

### HIGH-1: The ECF sweep base contradicts the new Waterfall Priority on three of four terms — falsifying the section's own "acyclic and total, no step depends on a later step" guarantee
- **Section:** `## Waterfall Priority` (lines 72–84, esp. step 5 line 80 and the acyclicity assertion line 84) vs. `## Cash Sweep Mechanics` — ECF definition (lines 103–105).
- **The claim:** Line 84 — *"The ordering is asserted acyclic and total: every dollar of CFADS is assigned to exactly one step, and no step depends on a later step."* Step 5 (line 80) is *"Mandatory prepayments and ECF cash sweep … ECF as defined in Cash Sweep Mechanics."* That definition (line 104) is **`ECF = CFADS − scheduled debt service − required reserve funding/replenishment − permitted distributions − permitted capex (growth/discretionary only)`.** This formula was carried over verbatim from `68c7ed6`; the resolution edited only its *reserve* term. Its other four terms were never checked against the seven-step order the same commit introduced.
- **Why it's wrong (three independent reconciliation failures; the reserve term is the *only* one that is consistent):**
  1. **Permitted distributions — the acyclicity violation.** The ECF formula *subtracts* permitted distributions (line 104), which **reduces the sweep base**, i.e., shelters that cash *from* the sweep — treating permitted distributions as **senior** to the sweep. The waterfall ranks **permitted equity distributions at step 6 (line 81), below the ECF sweep at step 5** — **junior** to it. The same bucket cannot be both above and below the sweep. And computing the step-5 sweep now *requires knowing* the step-6 distribution figure — a step depending on a later step, which is exactly what line 84 swears cannot happen. Under a covenant lock-up this is masked (distributions = 0, so `ECF − 0`), so it will pass a naive test; **outside lock-up it lets a permitted-distribution basket escape the sweep the waterfall declares senior to it**, diverting cash to equity that the stated priority sends to debt paydown. The term is also overloaded: line 81 folds the *senior* SPV-tax carve-out and *junior* discretionary distributions into one "permitted distributions" bucket, and the ECF subtracts the whole thing.
  2. **Growth capex and senior fees — the "total" violation, in both directions.** The ECF subtracts `permitted capex (growth/discretionary)`, and CFADS is net of *maintenance* capex only (line 141), so growth capex is a **real cash use that has no rung anywhere in the seven-step waterfall** — violating "every dollar of CFADS is assigned to exactly one step." Conversely, **step 1 (senior fees / admin expenses, line 77) is a waterfall rung that the ECF formula does *not* subtract**, so the ECF base is *overstated* by the fee amount → the sweep is sized on cash that was already spent on fees. One term is in the formula but not the waterfall; another is in the waterfall but not the formula.
  3. **"Scheduled debt service" scope is unspecified against step 4 → oversweep.** The waterfall ranks **mezzanine/subordinate debt service at step 4 (line 79), above the ECF sweep at step 5.** For the sweep base to be consistent, the ECF's `scheduled debt service` term **must** include mezz. The formula says only "scheduled debt service." If read as senior-only, the ECF overstates the sweepable pool by the entire mezz service and the sweep tries to divert cash already committed to mezz.
- **Worked failure (limbs 1 + 3, outside lock-up):** CFADS = 100; senior fees = 3; senior DS = 40; required reserve funding = 10; mezz DS = 20; permitted distribution basket = 12; growth capex = 0. Cash actually reaching step 5 after steps 1–4 = 100 − 3 − 40 − 10 − 20 = **27**. ECF per line 104 (reading "scheduled debt service" as senior-only, and subtracting the distribution basket) = 100 − 40 − 10 − 12 − 0 = **38**. At a 100% leverage-band sweep, the engine sizes a sweep of **38 against 27 of real cash** — a 41% oversweep — *and* the 12 distribution basket has been shielded from a sweep the waterfall says outranks it. Every discrepancy points the same way the acyclicity claim says is impossible.
- **Why it's HIGH (not CRITICAL, not MEDIUM):** Not CRITICAL — unlike the pass-3 defect, this does **not** corrupt the DSCR/LLCR/PLCR numerators (those read off clean CFADS and are unaffected). It is confined to sweep sizing and the debt-vs-equity cash split. But it is a genuine **contradiction between two LOCKED sections on cash priority**, it is **self-inflicted by the resolution's own new section**, it **falsifies that section's headline guarantee**, and it changes real dollars of debt repayment. That is materially more than a wording gap. It blocks GO.
- **Direction:** Reconcile the ECF formula to the seven-step order, term by term. (a) Decide whether permitted distributions are senior or junior to the sweep and make the two sections agree — if junior (as step 6 states), **remove** the `− permitted distributions` term from ECF; if a protected basket is intended, move that basket above the sweep in the waterfall and rename it so it is not confused with the step-6 discretionary distribution. (b) Give growth/discretionary capex an explicit waterfall rung (or state it is funded from a capex reserve/facility, not CFADS, and drop it from the ECF deductions). (c) Add senior fees (step 1) to the ECF deductions. (d) State that "scheduled debt service" in ECF means all debt service ranking above the sweep — senior **and** mezzanine (steps 2 + 4). Then re-assert acyclicity only after the formula and the ladder match.

- **Section:** `## Waterfall Priority` (line 74 header, step 5 line 80) vs. `## Mandatory Prepayments` (lines 116–121).
