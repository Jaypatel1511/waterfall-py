## MEDIUM

### MED-1 (NEW, self-inflicted by the pass-5 MED fix): The step-6 lock-up rule is internally contradictory — under either reading of how a cash-trap interacts with the sweep, the pass-5 additions collide
- **Section:** `## Waterfall Priority` step 6 (line 82, **rewritten this resolution**) vs. step 7 (line 83), the sweep types (line 106), and the three-tier cushion (line 101).
- **The issue:** The pass-5 MED fix added (a) an internal sub-order 6a→6b→6c with caps, where *"cash beyond a binding cap flows to the next sub-step, then to step 7,"* (b) *"6a–6c are blocked entirely under a covenant lock-up / cash-trap,"* and (c) an exception: *"excluded SPV tax distributions, if supplied as inputs, are permitted at 6c even during lock-up."* These do not reconcile with what a cash-trap *is*:
  - **If a lock-up/cash-trap operates by forcing the ECF sweep (step 5) to 100%** (the natural reading of line 101's *"trap (trigger — cash trap / sweep)"* and line 106's *"event-triggered (covenant breach)"* sweep type), then retained ECF `(1 − sweep%) × ECF = 0`, so **nothing reaches step 6 at all** — and the carve-out permitting SPV tax distributions "at 6c even during lock-up" is **unfundable**: there is no retained ECF to pay them from. The exception the fix added can never fire.
  - **If the lock-up does *not* force a 100% sweep** (it merely "blocks" 6a–6c), then retained ECF > 0 reaches step 6, 6a–6c are blocked, and per the fix's own rule that capped/blocked cash *"flows to the next sub-step, then to step 7,"* the blocked cash **falls through to step 7 = "Residual cash to equity."** That routes trapped cash to equity during a covenant lock-up — the precise outcome a cash-trap exists to prevent. Step 7 (residual to equity) is never stated to be blocked under lock-up; only 6a–6c are.
- **Why MEDIUM not HIGH:** it mis-specifies the *discretionary junior* region (below the sweep, above/at residual equity); it cannot mis-allocate cash between senior debt and anything else (the sweep already captured the senior share). But it is a genuine contradiction — a lender reviewing this would not accept "blocked junior cash leaks to equity in a trap," and the tax-distribution carve-out is dead under the other reading. It is also self-inflicted by the pass-5 MED fix, the same pattern the lineage keeps repeating.
- **Direction:** State explicitly (a) whether a lock-up forces the sweep to 100% (if so, delete or relocate the "tax distributions permitted at 6c during lock-up" carve-out, since step 6 is unfunded — put the tax-distribution carve-out at the sweep/step-5 level instead), and (b) that **step 7 residual-to-equity is *also* blocked/trapped under a lock-up** (trapped cash prepays debt or is held, never reaching equity). Do not leave "blocked → next sub-step → step 7" as the disposition for lock-up-blocked cash.
